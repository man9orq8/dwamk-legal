To help us investigate a problem, please include:

  1. A brief description of the problem and what you expected to happen.
  2. The steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.
  3. Your device model, iOS version and Dwamk app version.
  4. A screenshot if helpful, with any personal details hidden.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a work schedule?

Choose a work schedule type in Dwamk, then add or edit your shifts through the schedule section.

How do I import a university timetable?

Choose the university schedule option and import an image or PDF. Review the recognized classes, dates and times before saving.

Why didn't my alarm or reminder sound?

Check the scheduled date and time, relevant permissions and your device's sound settings. System conditions can affect alerts, so consider a backup alarm for important events.

How does sleep sound recording work?

With your permission, Dwamk can analyze audio during a sleep session on your device to identify sounds such as snoring or coughing. Results are estimates and are not medical diagnoses. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How can I restore a purchase?

Use the Restore Purchases option in the app's purchase settings, if available, while using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Contact support if you need further assistance.

How do I request a refund?

App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

How do I suggest a feature?

Send your idea to support@dwamk.com.
