Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Dwamk handles your data. The app is designed to process your data on your device whenever possible.

Data Controller

Dwamk is provided by Mansour Alajmi (MansourDev). For privacy inquiries, email support@dwamk.com.

Local Data

Your work schedule settings, schedules, and preferences are stored on your device. They may be shared locally with app extensions, such as widgets and the watch app, to provide the features you choose.

Microphone and Sleep

When you start a sleep session and give your permission, the app uses the microphone only during that session to detect snoring, coughing, and possible sleep talking on your device. Speech alone is not considered evidence of sleep. Speech detection requires prior signs of sleep and a short phrase followed by silence. Detection may be inaccurate or may miss sleep talking without snoring. Snoring and coughing detection continues while clips are playing, and recordings may include audio from playback, while sleep talking is excluded during playback. Accepted clips are saved automatically. They are approximately 10 seconds long, with a maximum of 20 automatically saved clips per session. Recordings are encrypted locally. The default retention period is 20 nights, which can be changed to 100 nights or permanent retention. Favorites are not deleted automatically, and audio is not uploaded to a server.

Apple Watch and Widgets

Selected schedule information, preferences, and alarm status may be made available to the iPhone widget and paired Apple Watch through Apple app-group storage and device connectivity. Their availability depends on pairing, device settings, and system permissions.

Calendar, Notifications, and Alarms

The app uses these permissions only to add the work shifts you select, send reminders, and trigger alarms. It does not modify calendar events that it did not create.

iPhone Calendar Synchronization

If you enable calendar synchronization, Dwamk requests access to the relevant calendar functionality to create and update selected work shifts. It may remove calendar events previously created by Dwamk when you disable synchronization or change your schedule; it is not intended to alter unrelated events.

Photos and Files

To import a schedule, the app processes the image or PDF file that you choose. Using the system picker does not give the app general access to your photo library.

Schedule Import and On-Device Assistance

Selected schedule images and PDFs can be analyzed on the device using document recognition and local assistance. Recognized entries may be incorrect, so you can review them before saving. This description does not imply that every future AI feature will necessarily work offline.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The app does not receive your bank card details.

Optional Usage Analytics

Usage analytics sharing is disabled by default. If you enable it in Settings, Dwamk uses Firebase Analytics to send general events about feature usage, operation results, and purchases. These may include your general schedule type, such as morning, shifts, rotations, or university; a random app installation identifier; your device type and operating system and app versions; and an approximate region inferred from your network address. Schedule contents or times, recordings, health data, your name, and your email address are not sent. You can disable sharing at any time, and your choice is stored on your device. These analytics are not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites.

Third-Party Analytics Provider

When you opt in to usage analytics, Google Firebase Analytics processes the permitted analytics information as a service provider. Analytics processing may involve infrastructure outside your country. For Google's product privacy information, visit Firebase Privacy and Security. Turning off analytics prevents subsequent app-initiated analytics events, but does not necessarily erase information already received by Google. Contact support for privacy requests concerning previously collected information.

Control and Deletion

You can disable permissions in your device settings and delete stored data using the tools available within the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Data Retention and Requests

Local schedules and settings remain until you change or remove them or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and storage behavior. Sleep recording retention is governed by the options described above. Analytics data may be retained by the service provider according to its applicable settings and policies. You can contact support@dwamk.com to ask questions about privacy or applicable data rights. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete data already sent to third parties.

Tracking

The app does not use targeted advertising or track your activity across other apps and websites.

Children

Dwamk is a scheduling and productivity app, not designed specifically for children. Users and guardians should review device permissions and the app's sleep recording features before use.

Policy Changes

We may revise this policy when features or data practices change. The last-updated date on this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For questions and support: support@dwamk.com
